A fast delivery doesn't depend solely on the rider or the company organizing it: it also depends, to a large extent, on how the order is prepared and communicated at the outset. An imprecise address, an unreachable phone, or a poorly defined order can turn a fifteen-minute run into a real headache, even for the most responsive team.

At Tiziwiz Delivery, we see every day that the fastest deliveries are also the ones that are best prepared upfront. So here are our practical tips, designed to help you get a **fast delivery in Agadir**, with no surprises or wasted time.

## Why the speed of a delivery also depends on you

A rider can only work with the information they're given. A clear address, a well-defined need, and good phone availability let the run proceed without interruption, from the first contact through to the handoff. Conversely, every missing or approximate piece of information generates an extra back-and-forth, an additional call, or a wait — all of which add unnecessary minutes to the final timeframe.

These few simple tips often make all the difference between a smooth delivery and one that drags on.

## Give a precise, complete address

This is arguably the most important point. In Agadir, some newer neighborhoods, certain residential complexes, or certain side streets can be hard to locate from a vague address. To avoid wasting time:

- State the exact name of the neighborhood (downtown, Founty, Sonaba, Dakhla, Sala, Tikiouine, etc.).
- Specify the building number, floor, and apartment number if needed.
- Add an easily identifiable landmark: a well-known shop, an intersection, a specific entrance.

A complete address from the very first message lets our team direct the nearest rider straight away, with no need for a clarifying back-and-forth.

### Gated residences, offices, and secured buildings

Gated residences, offices with controlled access, or guarded buildings deserve special attention. If an entry code, a doorman, or a prior phone call is needed to reach your address, mention it right when you place your order. This simple precaution keeps the rider from being stuck at the entrance waiting for access, which can easily add several minutes to the delivery.

## Stay reachable during the delivery window

Even with a precise address, a rider may need to reach you: to confirm one last detail, to be guided through a residential complex, or simply to let you know they've arrived. Staying available on your phone during the estimated delivery window avoids back-and-forth outside a building and minutes lost waiting for a reply.

If you know in advance that you'll be hard to reach (a meeting, a class, an appointment), it's best to mention it when placing your order, so we can plan ahead — for example by agreeing on an alternative handoff spot or a secondary contact.

## Communicate your specific needs from the start

Some orders call for extra care: a dish that needs to stay flat, a fragile item, a discreet delivery for a sensitive prescription, or a precise time window because you'll only be available for a short slot. Rather than assuming these details are obvious, it's better to state them clearly when placing the order. A rider who's informed in advance can adjust how they carry and hand over the order, avoiding unpleasant surprises on arrival.

## Prepare your order details in advance

The more clearly an order is defined from the outset, the faster it can be processed. This varies depending on the type of delivery requested.

### For a meal delivery

Before contacting us, have in mind the exact name of the restaurant, the dish or dishes you want, and any specifications (no onions, sauce on the side, etc.). These details let our [meal delivery service](/services/livraison-de-repas) pass along a complete order the very first time we're in the kitchen, without needing to come back to you partway through.

### For a grocery delivery

Prepare a clear list, ideally with quantities and preferred brands whenever that matters to you. Our [grocery delivery service](/services/livraison-de-courses) can then select your products directly, without extra back-and-forth to clear up any ambiguity.

### For a medication delivery

Have the exact name of the medications on hand, or a photo of the prescription, along with the name of your preferred pharmacy if you have one. This lets our [medication delivery service](/services/livraison-de-medicaments) act immediately, which matters especially in urgent situations, day or night.

## Have your payment method ready

One last detail, often overlooked, can still slow down the handoff: payment. Ask about accepted payment methods as soon as you make contact, and if you're paying in cash, prepare exact change if possible. This small habit avoids extra waiting time at handoff, especially when several deliveries follow one another in the same neighborhood.

## The mistakes that most often slow down a delivery

Some habits come up regularly and needlessly stretch out delivery times. Here are the main ones to avoid:

- Giving an approximate address, with no specific neighborhood or landmark.
- Being unreachable by phone right when the rider tries to contact you.
- Giving an incomplete order, planning to fill in the details later.
- Not mentioning a change of address or order as soon as it happens.
- Forgetting to mention special access (a code, a doorman, a floor with no elevator).

Avoiding these few pitfalls is, in most cases, enough to guarantee a smooth delivery from start to finish.

## Orders for several people or for an office

When an order is placed for several people — a group meal, groceries for the whole family, an order shared between colleagues — advance preparation matters even more. It's best to designate a single person responsible for passing along the complete, consolidated list, rather than letting several requests arrive separately and sometimes inconsistently. That same person should stay reachable and be available at the moment of handoff, to avoid any confusion once the rider arrives.

This organization, simple as it sounds, prevents most of the misunderstandings that can arise with this type of order, whether it's lunch among colleagues or a shopping list shared between several members of a household.

## Allow a reasonable time margin for complex orders

Not all deliveries require the same preparation time. A shopping list with dozens of items, or an order involving several pickup addresses, naturally takes longer than a simple run. Keeping that in mind helps set realistic expectations and avoid unnecessary frustration: our team always stays transparent about the estimated timeframe as soon as the order is confirmed, so you know exactly what to expect.

## What's the best time to order a fast delivery?

Delivery times can vary slightly depending on the time of day, as with any activity in the city. Mid-morning and mid-afternoon slots are generally the smoothest, while mealtimes (around midday and in the evening) naturally see more demand, particularly for meal delivery.

That doesn't mean you should avoid those hours — Tiziwiz Delivery remains available 24/7 regardless of the time — but if your need isn't urgent, ordering slightly before or after the usual peaks can get you an even shorter timeframe. For genuinely urgent needs, our [express delivery service](/services/livraison-express) is designed to respond quickly at any time, including during busy periods.

## Check your order when it arrives

Once the rider arrives, take a few seconds to check that the order matches what you were expecting before they leave: the right number of items for a grocery delivery, the right dish for a meal, the right box of medication for a prescription. This simple habit lets you flag any issue immediately, while the rider is still there, rather than having to arrange a second run afterward to fix a mistake.

## Plan ahead for recurring needs

If you regularly order the same type of products — a weekly shopping list, a monthly prescription refill — it can help to keep that standard list on hand, ready to send in a few seconds. This simple habit considerably speeds up placing an order and reduces the risk of forgetting an item, while saving you time with every new request.
