## How to prepare a good shopping list for a rider

A well-prepared grocery order makes the rider's job much easier and reduces the risk of misunderstandings. A few good practices help you get a result closer to what you would have chosen yourself in the store.

- **Be precise about brands and quantities**, especially for products where several options exist (brand, size, quantity).
- **Specify the store or market you want**, if you have a particular preference for certain products.
- **Flag your priorities in case of unavailability** — for example, whether a product is essential or an alternative is acceptable.
- **Send your whole list in a single message**, rather than adding items one at a time, so the rider can organize their store visit efficiently.
- **State your approximate budget** if you have one, particularly for fresh produce whose price can vary.

A clear list, sent by WhatsApp or given by phone, lets our team select your products with care, as if you were there in the aisles yourself.

## Mistakes to avoid for a hassle-free grocery delivery

A few recurring inaccuracies can slow down or complicate a grocery order:

- **A list that's too vague**, like "some fruit" or "dairy products" with no further detail, forcing the rider to guess your preferences.
- **No indication of which store**, when the choice of retailer can affect the availability and price of certain products.
- **An unreachable phone** right when the rider needs to confirm a substitution.
- **Forgetting the approximate budget**, particularly for fresh produce whose price can vary from one visit to the next.

Avoiding these few pitfalls helps you receive groceries closer to what you would have chosen yourself, while making the rider's job easier.
