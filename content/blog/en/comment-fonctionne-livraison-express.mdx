## What does "express delivery" actually mean?

The word "express" is sometimes used loosely. At Tiziwiz SARL, it corresponds to a precise way of working: as soon as a request for [express delivery](/services/livraison-express) is confirmed, a rider is mobilized immediately and devoted solely to that run, without being folded into a route covering several orders.

That distinction changes everything. On a standard delivery, a rider may string together several nearby runs to make the most of their route — which works perfectly well for most everyday needs. On an express delivery, however, the goal is different: get from point A to point B as directly as possible, with a single priority in mind — speed.

## How express delivery differs from standard delivery

The key difference lies in how the rider is assigned. On a standard run, the same rider can handle several orders in parallel, which remains efficient for needs that aren't under a strict time constraint. On an express run, a single rider is assigned to a single order from start to finish, which mechanically cuts down waiting time.

This approach carries a higher logistical cost — mobilizing a rider for a single run is less efficient than a grouped route — but in exchange it guarantees a speed that few other formats can match.
