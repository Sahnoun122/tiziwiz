## Our tips for ordering a meal well in Agadir

A well-prepared meal order arrives faster and matches what you were expecting more closely. A few good practices make every order even smoother:

- **Specify the exact restaurant and dishes**, including any options or customizations, to avoid any misunderstanding.
- **Give a clear address**, with a landmark if needed, especially in residential neighborhoods that are less obvious to locate.
- **Plan ahead for group orders**: for an office or a large family, it's simpler to send all the dishes in a single message rather than several separate ones.
- **Check the opening hours of the restaurant you've chosen**, particularly for late orders, to make sure it's still open.
- **Stay reachable** during preparation and delivery, for any last-minute clarification.
- **Mention any allergy or dietary restriction** directly when placing the order, so the restaurant is informed before preparation.
- **Allow a little extra time during busy hours**, particularly around lunch and dinner, when restaurant kitchens are under more pressure.
