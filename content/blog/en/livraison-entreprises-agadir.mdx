## Why a business in Agadir needs a delivery partner

Managing delivery in-house is often a heavier burden than expected. You have to recruit riders, manage their schedules, insure their scooters, absorb slow periods and activity spikes, and stay reachable when something unexpected comes up. For a business whose core activity isn't logistics, these constraints end up weighing on the main business, without necessarily providing a more reliable service to the end customer.

Turning to an outside partner like Tiziwiz SARL flips this logic: the business focuses on what it does — cooking, selling, caring for patients, administration — while delivery is handled by a dedicated team, available 24/7, able to absorb swings in activity without putting service quality at risk.

## The hidden costs of an in-house delivery fleet

Beyond a rider's salary, building an in-house fleet involves a series of costs that are often invisible at first: buying or renting scooters, maintaining them, insurance, plus the management time needed to organize schedules, handle absences, or replace a sick rider. For a small or medium-sized business, this management time represents a mental and operational load that weighs directly on the core business.

On top of that comes the question of continuity: what happens on a particularly busy day, when the usual rider is unavailable? An outside partner like Tiziwiz SARL naturally absorbs these swings, since our team is structured specifically to handle activity spikes, without that becoming the client business's problem.

## One-off delivery or recurring delivery: which plan should you choose?

Not all businesses have the same needs, which is why Tiziwiz SARL offers two complementary approaches.

Some businesses only need a rider occasionally: an urgent document, an unexpected activity spike, a quick fix. In that case, simply contact us when the need arises, exactly as an individual would, and a run will be handled quickly.

For businesses whose activity generates a regular delivery need — a restaurant delivering several times a day, a shop shipping parcels daily — we set up a recurring arrangement: reserved time slots, volume-based pricing, and simplified coordination with a familiar contact rather than a fresh exchange for every order.
