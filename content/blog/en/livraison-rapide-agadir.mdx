## Standard delivery or express delivery: how to choose?

Not every delivery carries the same urgency, and it isn't always necessary to pay for priority handling. Here's how to tell the difference:

- **Standard delivery**: ideal for your weekly groceries, a meal ordered with no particular urgency, or any request that can tolerate a slightly more flexible timeframe.
- **Express delivery**: recommended whenever a precise deadline must be met — a professional appointment, a last-minute order, or a one-off need that can't wait.

If in doubt, the simplest approach is to tell us your time constraint when you contact us: our team will point you directly toward the most suitable option, without you having to choose between the two formats yourself.

## Mistakes that can slow down a delivery

Even with an efficient organization, certain details on the customer's side can unintentionally add to the timeframe. Being aware of them makes them easy to avoid.

- **An incomplete or approximate address** often forces the rider to call to confirm the route, wasting valuable time.
- **An unreachable phone** right when the rider needs to confirm a detail can put the run on hold while they try to reach you.
- **A request sent without flagging its urgency** can lead to your order being treated as a standard delivery rather than an express priority.
- **Payment not ready** when the rider arrives, adding a few unnecessary minutes to the final handoff.

These situations remain rare, but they show just how much the speed of a delivery depends on good coordination between the customer and the rider, not solely on the service's own organization.

## Our tips for getting your order even faster

Even with an efficient organization on our end, a few simple habits can save you even more time:

- **Be precise about the address**: mention the neighborhood, a visible landmark, and, if needed, the floor or apartment number in your very first message.
- **Stay reachable**: an available phone during the run avoids unnecessary back-and-forth if the rider needs a clarification.
- **Have your payment ready in advance** so the rider isn't left waiting on arrival.
- **Combine your needs when possible**: a run that covers several nearby stops is often faster than multiple separate small requests.
- **Plan ahead during busy hours** in the late afternoon, when traffic can slightly lengthen timeframes.

These few simple habits, combined with our organization, make all the difference to the actual delivery time. More practical recommendations are covered in our article on [tips for fast delivery](/blog/conseils-livraison-rapide).
